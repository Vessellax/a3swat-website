Seasonal Fundraiser

Help Build the Future of A3 SWAT

At A3 SWAT Baseball, our mission is to develop elite baseball players while building disciplined, accountable young men through attitude, effort, and competition. To continue providing a premier development experience for our athletes, we're asking for the support of our families, friends, local businesses, and community.

Where Your Donations Go

Reinvested Directly in Our Athletes

Every dollar raised helps our players grow as both athletes and young men.

Training Equipment
High-quality training equipment and player development resources.

Facility Improvements
Field and facility upgrades that create a better training environment.
03
Scholarships
Financial assistance and scholarships for families in need.
04
Showcase Opportunities
Exposure events that help players continue pursuing their baseball goals.

Fundraising Goal Per Player

Each player is encouraged to raise $300 by reaching out to family, friends, neighbors, coworkers, and local businesses. Every contribution — large or small — helps us continue building one of the premier youth baseball programs in Texas.
